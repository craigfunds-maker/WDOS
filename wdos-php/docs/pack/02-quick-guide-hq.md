# 02. Quick guide for HQ staff

WDOS opens in a browser on a phone or a computer. Address of the test copy: https://wdos.onrender.com. The menu names below are the ones on the screen.

## First time

1. Open the address. Choose your language.
2. Press Sign in. Type the email and the temporary password a super administrator gave you in person.
3. WDOS asks for a new password. Choose your own. Nobody else can read it.
4. In Settings, add this device so you can sign in with your fingerprint next time, and switch notifications on.

## Every day (about ten minutes)

| Look at | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| New members and the two networks | Dashboard | Nothing. It counts by itself. |
| Exceptions | Dashboard, Exceptions for HQ | Open each one. Clear it or keep it under review. This is the only part of joining that a person looks at. |
| Messages from members and leaders | Messages | Reply. Replies carry your name. Mark resolved. |
| Private reports | Confidential cases | Open, write the outcome the member will read, update the status. |
| WNNN members reaching 30 | Dashboard card | Each is told automatically. Moving a member to WGMN is a person's decision (open decision D08). |

## Things you will do often

- **Appoint a leader.** Members, open the member, Appoint to a seat. Choose the position and the area. The member receives the Agreement and onboarding.
- **Set the end of a term.** On the member's page, Term of this appointment. HQ is told 30 days before and on the day.
- **Send an announcement.** Communications or Announcements, New announcement. Choose the audience down to one chapter or one member. Add other languages if you have them. Send now or schedule.
- **Schedule a meeting or a talk show.** Meetings. Everyone invited is told and reminded automatically.
- **Help a locked-out member.** Members, open the member, New temporary password. Give it in person or by a private message, never in a group.
- **Open a chapter by hand.** Chapters. Normally WDOS opens chapters by itself when one is full.
- **Read reports.** Reports for live counts. Leader reports for what leaders wrote.

## Things only a super administrator does

Staff and logins (create or switch off a staff login), give or remove HQ access, and Dashboard, Operations, Take a backup now.

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Please sign in to continue" | The session ended | Sign in again |
| A page says it did not load | Weak connection | Wait, then reload. Nothing is lost. |
| A member says she received nothing | Phone notifications may be off on her phone | Communications shows delivery. "Sent" does not mean "read". |
| A number looks wrong | Counts come from records | Handoff register, Live integrity checks. Each should be none. Tell IT if not. |

## Rules to keep

- Never share a login. Each person has one.
- Never paste passwords or private case details into a message, a task or a report.
- The backup file holds every member's details. Keep it private and off shared drives.
