# 05. Video scripts for HQ, leaders and IT

Scripts only. The member videos already exist inside WDOS. These three have not been recorded. Plain sentences, one action per sentence, so they can be translated and voiced. Each scene names the real screen to show. Use test accounts with invented names when recording; never a real member's details.

## Video A. HQ staff: your first day in WDOS (about 4 minutes)

| Scene | Screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Language list, then Sign in | "Open WDOS. Choose your language. Press Sign in." |
| 2 | Choose your own password | "Type the temporary password you were given. Now choose your own. Nobody else can read it." |
| 3 | Dashboard | "This is your dashboard. New members, the two networks and the countries are counted for you. You do not type any totals." |
| 4 | Exceptions for HQ | "Most women join and become active with no staff step. Only exceptions wait here. Open one. Clear it, or keep it under review." |
| 5 | Messages | "Members and leaders write to HQ here. Reply. Your name is on the reply. Mark it resolved." |
| 6 | Members, a member page | "Find any member. To make her a leader, press Appoint to a seat, choose the position and the area." |
| 7 | New announcement | "To tell people something, write an announcement. Choose who receives it, down to one chapter. Send now or choose a time." |
| 8 | Confidential cases | "Private reports come only to HQ. Write the outcome the member will read. Never copy a case into a message." |
| 9 | Closing | "Sign out when you finish. Never share your login." |

## Video B. Volunteer leaders: your leadership dashboard (about 4 minutes)

| Scene | Screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notice of appointment | "HQ has appointed you. Sign in the way you always do." |
| 2 | Onboarding | "Open Onboarding. Read the Agreement to the end and accept it. Then finish each step in order." |
| 3 | Dashboard | "This is your area. You see your own network and the places under you." |
| 4 | Chapters, one chapter | "Open a chapter. You see how full it is and who is waiting nearby. A chapter holds thirty to fifty members. When it is full, WDOS opens the next one." |
| 5 | Record activity | "When you hold an activity, record it the same day. It goes into your monthly report by itself." |
| 6 | My reports | "Each month, open your report, check it and submit. Your supervisor approves it or sends it back with a reason." |
| 7 | Tasks | "Tasks given to you are here. Finish the checklist and submit." |
| 8 | Meetings | "Schedule a meeting. Everyone invited is told and reminded. Afterwards mark who attended and write what was decided." |
| 9 | Help and private reports | "A complaint or a safeguarding concern goes here, to HQ only. Never in a task or a report." |

## Video C. IT: release, check, back up (about 3 minutes)

| Scene | Screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Repository and Render | "WDOS code is in the repository. Render builds and runs it. The database is TiDB Cloud." |
| 2 | Test output | "Before a release, run the tests. Every suite must say failures zero." |
| 3 | Render deploy | "Push to main and deploy. Wait for the deploy to finish." |
| 4 | `/api/config`, then the site | "Open api config. It must answer. Then open the site and sign in." |
| 5 | Handoff register | "As HQ, open the Handoff register. Migration error must be empty. Integrity checks must all say none." |
| 6 | Operations, backup | "A super administrator takes a backup here with their own password. Store the file somewhere private, off the server." |
| 7 | Render, earlier deploys | "If a release is bad, redeploy the last good one. Then find the cause." |
