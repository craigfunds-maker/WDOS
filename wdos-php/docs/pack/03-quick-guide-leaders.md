# 03. Quick guide for volunteer leaders (WGMN Coordinators and WNNN Leads)

You see your own area and your own network only. That is how WDOS is built, not a fault.

## Getting started

1. HQ appoints you. You receive a notice in WDOS.
2. Sign in the way you joined: your email or your phone number, and your password.
3. Open Onboarding. Read and accept the Volunteer Leadership Agreement, then work through each step in order.
4. Your Dashboard opens when onboarding is complete.

## Your menu

| Menu | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Your area at a glance: members, chapters, tasks, what needs you |
| My members | Members in your area and network |
| Chapters, My chapter | Each chapter: how full it is, who is in it, who is waiting nearby |
| Who holds each seat | Seats under you, filled or vacant |
| Referral links | A link for each vacant seat under you. Share it. HQ decides the appointment. |
| Tasks | Work given to you, and work you give to others in your area |
| My reports | Your monthly report. Record activity as it happens and the report fills from it. |
| Meetings | Schedule, mark attendance, write the minutes. Actions become tasks. |
| Announcements | Send a notice to your area or to your chapter |
| Messages with HQ | Write to HQ and read the replies |

## The four things a leader does most

1. **Record an activity** the day it happens: My reports, Record activity. You are not asked for it again.
2. **Submit the monthly report.** WDOS reminds you before it is due. Your supervisor approves it or sends it back with a reason.
3. **Close your tasks.** Open the task, tick the checklist, submit. Some tasks need approval.
4. **Help a member of your own chapter who is with you and cannot sign in** (Chapter Leads and their assistants only, where HQ has left this switched on): on the chapter page, Reset PIN. The temporary PIN is shown once. HQ is told. You can do this five times a day at most.

## What a leader cannot do

Appoint or remove leaders, open or close chapters, move a member to another chapter, see the other network, see private reports, or see members outside the area. Ask HQ through Messages with HQ.

## Deputies, assistants and administrative secretaries

You work in the same area as the leader you support. You add your contribution to the unit report; the leader submits it. He-for-She Champions hold these seats only.

## If something is wrong

- A member is in the wrong chapter: tell HQ through Messages with HQ. HQ moves the member and the history is kept.
- A safeguarding matter or a complaint: never put it in a task, report or issue. Use Help and private reports. It goes to HQ only.
- Anything else: Messages with HQ.
