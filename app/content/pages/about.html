H.E. Zinariya Nneoma Nkechi Rochas Okorocha

Founder, Women of Divine Destiny Initiative (WODDI)

Driven by compassion and the belief that every life deserves dignity and opportunity, H.E. Zinariya Nneoma Nkechi Rochas Okorocha founded WODDI to nurture, empower and transform the lives of women, youth, adolescents and vulnerable individuals across Africa.

Through education, mentorship, skills acquisition, leadership development and humanitarian support, WODDI strengthens individuals, families and communities.

Her vision finds expression through the WODDI Good Mother Network (WGMN) for women aged 30 and above, and the WODDI Nurture NextGen Network (WNNN) for young women aged 18–29, alongside broader humanitarian initiatives.

The WODDI Institute advances this vision through free, accessible online learning, equipping women with the knowledge, values and skills to transform their lives, strengthen families and positively influence their communities.

WODDI Institute is the learning platform of the Women of Divine Destiny Initiative (WODDI), a women-focused organisation serving learners and volunteers across Africa and beyond.

What we offer

  • Practical courses in digital skills, business, personal growth and hands-on trades, written in short lessons you can finish on your phone.
  • Lessons in English, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo and Swahili, with the lesson text and audio in the language you choose.
  • A WODDI Institute certificate for each course you complete, with a QR code and a public check at woddiinstitute.com/verify.
  • An AI Mentor for questions, a support team, and a community of alumni.
  • Growth Points for the modules you finish, which you can use on the WODDI Marketplace.

Two networks, one Institute

  • WODDI Good Mother Network (WGMN) for women aged 30 and above, focusing on identity, family, leadership and community.
  • WODDI Nurture NextGen Network (WNNN) for young women aged 18 to 29, focusing on identity, leadership, career readiness and entrepreneurship.

How learning works

Sign in, choose a course, and work through its modules at your own pace. Your progress is saved, so you can pick up where you stopped on any phone. Each module ends with a short check, and a course ends with a final quiz.

Get in touch

Learners can reach the support team from inside the app. If you want a quick guide to getting back into your course, watch the learner videos.
