Executive Profile

Professor Nnamdi Okechukwu Nwaodu is a distinguished Professor of International Relations, policy strategist, development scholar, international conference convener, and institutional leader with over twenty-five years of academic, research, university administration, and public policy experience across Africa. He has made significant contributions to scholarship in International Relations, Public Administration, Development Studies, Governance, Diplomacy, Peace and Security Studies, Political Economy, Global Health, Healthy Ageing, Climate Justice, Circular Economy, and African Development.

He currently serves as Director General of the Centre for African American Research Studies (CAARS), President of the Good Health for All Society (GHAS), and Lead Advocate of the African Healthy Ageing and Wellness Initiative (AHAWI). He has also provided technical policy support to government institutions as Technical Adviser on Circular Economy and Development to the Bayelsa State Government.

Professor Nwaodu has supervised numerous doctoral and master's candidates, served as external examiner to universities in Nigeria and South Africa, authored and edited many scholarly books and journal articles, delivered keynote addresses across Africa, and convened several international conferences that have influenced public policy, governance, higher education, security, public health, and sustainable development.

International Research Leadership

Professor Nwaodu has demonstrated sustained leadership in multidisciplinary research and policy development across Africa through the Centre for African American Research Studies (CAARS). Under his leadership, CAARS has expanded research collaborations across Nigeria, South Africa, Rwanda, Canada, the United States, and other countries, promoting evidence-based policymaking, scholarly publishing, capacity development, and international dialogue on governance, peace, security, global health, climate justice, education, and sustainable development. He has led numerous international research initiatives and served as convener of major continental conferences, bringing together academics, policymakers, development practitioners, diplomats, industry leaders, and civil society organizations to address Africa's strategic development priorities.

International Academic, Research And Policy Leadership

Professor Nnamdi Okechukwu Nwaodu has established himself as one of Africa's foremost scholars in International Relations, Public Policy, Development Administration, Governance, Security Studies and African Development. His academic career spans more than twenty-five years of teaching, research, postgraduate supervision, institutional administration, policy advisory services and international academic collaboration. As Director General of the Centre for African American Research Studies (CAARS), he has provided visionary leadership in developing one of Africa's emerging multidisciplinary research and policy institutes, facilitating collaborations among universities, governments, professional bodies, development agencies and civil society organizations across Africa and the African Diaspora.

His leadership extends beyond academia into public policy, strategic development, global health, higher education transformation, climate justice, military professionalism, and healthy ageing advocacy.
