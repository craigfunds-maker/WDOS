Biography

Dr. Sani Alhaji Dangana

Chief Lecturer and Head of Section, Automobile Technology, Kaduna Polytechnic

Dr. Sani Alhaji Dangana is a distinguished teacher and researcher in Vocational Technical Education, specializing in Automobile Technology. His academic path began at the Institute and Faculty of Education, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, where he earned the NCE (Technical) in 1991 and a degree in Automobile Technology in 1995. He then went to the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, for his Master's degree (2006) and his Ph.D. in Automobile Electronics (2018).

At Kaduna Polytechnic he has risen to the rank of Chief Lecturer. He is Head of the Automobile Technology Section in the Department of Mechanical and Production Technology, and Coordinator of SIWES for the College of Technical and Vocational Education. He has served as Head of Section of Vocational and Technical Education (Automobile), Secretary to two Departmental Boards of Examiners, and as Coordinator of NCE Technical Practical Research.

His service reaches well beyond the Polytechnic. He has been External Examiner to the Federal Colleges of Education (Technical) in Bichi and Gusau, and to Isa Kaita College of Education, Dutsin-Ma. He was a member of the WASSC National Coordination on Auto Mechanic Works held in Ibadan, (2016–2017) and has chaired the NECO National Coordination on Auto Electrical Works since 2015. In February 2022 he also took part in a youth skills training and empowerment programme on Vocational Education in Jos, organized by the Federal Ministry of Information, Youth and Sports.

A member of the Teachers Registration Council, NATT, A.P.Q.E.N. and NACT, Dr. Dangana is respected for his commitment to Vocational and technical skills, sound examination standards, and the training of the next generation of technologists.
