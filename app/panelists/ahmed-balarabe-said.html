Biography

Ahmed Balarabe Sa’id is a communications strategist, lecturer, media and public relations professional with over 17 years of combined industry and academic experience.

He is currently on leave, serving as Senior Special Assistant on Strategic Communication to the Vice Chairman, House of Representatives Committee on Federal Polytechnics and Institutions of Higher Technical Education.

At Kaduna Polytechnic, he held several roles, including Departmental Staff Secretary; Head of Examinations and Records; pioneer Editor of the institution’s newsletter, Kadpoly Chronicle; and pioneer Manager and later Board Member of the institution’s Campus FM Radio.

His previous media career includes Reporter, Bureau Chief, and later Managing Editor at The Market Magazine; one-time News Editor at Independent Television Abuja; and Editor-at-Large at Newspage Newspaper and Valuechain Magazine.

He is the author of Aren’t We All PR People?, a Fellow of the Institute of Management Consultants, and an Adhoc member of the Resource Inspection Team for the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE).

He was among a team of 10 experts selected to review INEC’s Strategic Communication Framework and served as Team Lead for a World Bank-funded project on Radio Adoption by Irrigation Farmers.

A distinguished NTA Academic Prize winner, Ahmed was Chapter Secretary and Study Centre Coordinator of the Nigerian Institute of Public Relations and was recently appointed State Coordinator of the Society for Nigerian Broadcasters. He is the Faculty Head of Kaduna Media Academy and is currently pursuing a PhD in Strategic and Development Communication.
