Professor Ikechukwu Jude Donald Nwosu is a Professor of Political Science and International Relations at Imo State University, Owerri, Nigeria, where he currently serves as Dean of the Faculty of Social Sciences. He holds a PhD in Political Science (International Relations) from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka. With over 18 years of university teaching experience, Professor Nwosu has published over 40 scholarly works, including three major books, and supervised numerous undergraduate projects, master’s dissertations, and doctoral theses. He is a member of the Nigerian Political Science Association, New York State Political Science Association, the International Journal of Arts and Science, USA, and a fellow at the Centre for African-American Research Studies (CAARS). He also served at the International Advisory Board of the Sankalpa International Journal of Management Decision, India, and is a member of the Board of Regents of Benson Idahosa University, Nigeria. Professor Nwosu is a PhD and M.Sc. External Examiner to various universities, and NUC accreditation Chairman/panel member in several universities. He is a book reviewer and an assessor of professorial candidates. He has participated in international conferences at the University of Oxford, Buckinghamshire New University (BNU), Ryerson University in Canada, in Thailand and the United States. His research interests include international relations, diplomacy, foreign policy, legislative studies, leadership, governance, democratic accountability, policy analysis, peace and conflict studies and security studies. He is also a community leader and senior pastor with the Church of God Mission International. Most recently, in his capacity as the Dean of the Faculty of Social Sciences of Imo State University, Owerri in collaboration with CAARS, organized a Conference themed: Military Professionalism, Peace and Security in Africa: Repositioning Security Institutions for Democratic Stability and Global Responsibility, which attracted military experts, Government officials and scholars from within and outside Nigeria.
