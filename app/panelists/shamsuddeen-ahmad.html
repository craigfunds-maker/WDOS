Biography

Shamsuddeen Musa Ahmad is a Principal Lecturer at Kaduna Polytechnic, Nigeria, with extensive experience in higher education, computer science, information technology, and academic research. He holds an M.Sc. in Computer Science from Kaduna State University and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. He is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. In addition to his teaching and research responsibilities at Kaduna Polytechnic, he serves as a Visiting Lecturer at Maryam Abacha American University of Niger and Franco British International University, Kaduna. He also serves as an External Examiner at the Federal Polytechnic, Minna, Niger State, contributing to academic assessment and quality assurance in tertiary education.

Before joining Kaduna Polytechnic, Ahmad worked as a Computer Systems Administrator at Union Bank Nigeria Plc from 2008 to 2019, where he gained considerable experience in network administration, systems maintenance, troubleshooting, and data management. He also worked at Kaduna State University and undertook his National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) assignment with the ICT Department of the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), Kaduna.

His research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, cybersecurity, educational data analytics, and recommendation systems. His publications cover areas such as adaptive and explainable machine learning for proactive cybersecurity threat detection, student academic performance prediction, and research trends in recommendation systems. He has also presented papers at national and international conferences.

His professional achievements include obtaining the National Skills Qualification (NSQ) Level 3 Certificate in Quality Assurance of Assessment in ICT and becoming a member of the Nigeria Computer Society (NCS). His technical expertise encompasses database application development, computer networking, web development, systems administration, and computer maintenance.

He is committed to excellence in teaching, research, academic assessment, institutional service, and the application of emerging computing technologies to educational and societal challenges.
