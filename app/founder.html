H.E. Zinariya Nneoma Nkechi Rochas Okorocha

Founder, Women of Divine Destiny Initiative (WODDI)

Driven by compassion, purpose and an unwavering belief in human potential, H.E. Zinariya Nneoma Nkechi Rochas Okorocha founded the Women of Divine Destiny Initiative (WODDI) with a powerful vision: to nurture lives, restore dignity, unlock potential and create opportunities for women, youth, adolescents and vulnerable individuals across Africa.

Her mission is rooted in the conviction that every individual deserves an opportunity to thrive, regardless of background or circumstances. She believes that when women are empowered, families become stronger; when young people and adolescents are properly nurtured, the future is secured; and when vulnerable individuals are supported, communities become more inclusive, resilient and prosperous.

Through its programmes and initiatives, WODDI promotes empowerment, education, skills acquisition, mentorship, leadership development, family well-being and humanitarian support.

Central to this vision are the WODDI Good Mother Network (WGMN), serving women aged 30 and above, and the WODDI Nurture NextGen Network (WNNN), empowering young women aged 18–29. Alongside these networks, WODDI’s broader humanitarian initiatives address the needs of adolescents and vulnerable individuals.

The WODDI Institute advances this mission through free, accessible online learning, equipping women across Africa with the knowledge, values, practical skills and leadership capacity to transform their lives, strengthen their families, mentor future generations and positively influence their communities.

Her ultimate vision is to build a continent-wide movement of empowered women, purposeful young people, nurtured adolescents and supported vulnerable individuals, creating lasting transformation across generations.

Nurturing Lives. Empowering Generations. Transforming Africa.
