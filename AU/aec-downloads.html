Statistics

2025 federal election participation rates

The 2025 federal election had a record enrolment rate and is on track for voter turnout that is slightly above the previous federal election in 2022. Detailed information is available on the participation in the election.

Enrolment statistics

Enrolment statistics are updated regularly prior to a federal election via a dedicated enrolment statistics section. After the close of rolls deadline, these statistics will be updated.

Previous elections and operational information

The AEC’s reporting guide for the 2025 federal election includes a range of high level statistics from previous federal elections as well as information about operational delivery for this election.

In addition, further information and statistics from previous federal elections is available.

Postal voting

The below file indicates the number of postal vote applications received by the AEC and the number of postal votes returned to the AEC.

This data will be updated on this page daily.

  • Postal vote applications (21 May 2025)

Postal vote applications close on Wednesday, 30 April 2025 6pm local time.

Pre-poll voting

The data in this spreadsheet includes all the pre-poll votes cast at pre-poll voting centres (PPVCs) for the 2025 federal election. The spreadsheet includes a breakdown of the number of pre-poll votes issued at every PPVC for every day of the pre-poll voting period. The votes issued include both ordinary and declaration votes. Declaration votes are grouped and counted separately by type.

  • Pre-poll votes (9 May 2025)

SA1 vote statistics

These files provide an indicative calculation of House of Representatives votes cast per SA1 at the 2025 federal election. This data is available in a single national file, or split into individual state files. Note that the national file is very large; care should be taken when opening this file using certain software products as some data may not be loaded correctly.

This data assists in the analysis of the geographic footprint of polling places. It uses roll mark-off data combined with polling place results to provide data by SA1s (the CCD column). It is a statistical dataset that is not directly comparable to vote counts due to rounding occurring in the data preparation.

This data uses the Australian Statistical Geography Standard (ASGS) edition 3 (2021). Comparison to other ASGS editions may require concordance.

  • Votes by SA1 – national
  • Votes by SA1 – by state

Mobile and remote mobile teams

The data in this spreadsheet includes all votes issued by each mobile polling team (including remote voter services teams), for the 2025 federal election. The votes issued include both ordinary and declaration votes. Declaration votes are grouped and counted separately by type. For information on the location and timing of mobile team visits, the mobile polling services spreadsheet can be used.

  • Mobile and remote polling (9 May 2025)
